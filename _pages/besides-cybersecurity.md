## Developer

Besides cybersecurity, I have developed some websites.

1. **[nyekusa.github.io](https://nyekusa.github.io)** — I developed this for our campus club. I felt we needed a platform to express who we are and what we do. I also added a functionality to bookmark the photos of our events and our members.

2. **[findawesomegames.vercel.app](https://findawesomegames.vercel.app)** — This is a repository of games to play — for team-building activities, organisations, outdoors, and even for couples. I always try my best to update the repo to have as many games as possible. This idea came when, in campus, we had a games session and nobody had a perfect game to play at the time. So I thought: how about I make a games repo?

3. **[awesomeai-chat.vercel.app](https://awesomeai-chat.vercel.app)** — One thing about me is I love being happy. So I made an intentionally hallucinating AI — just for jokes.
