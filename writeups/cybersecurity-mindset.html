Cybersecurity Mindset

Knowledge is power. With cybersecurity knowledge, you have a choice: be the good guy, or be the bad guy. But here's the real question — do you want to spend your life looking over your shoulder because of one bad decision made with that knowledge?

I've settled on an answer: life is genuinely better spent building and protecting than exploiting for personal gain. That mindset shapes every engagement I take on.
