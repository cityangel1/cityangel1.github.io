Port Scanning — Nmap Lab

Nmap

Port scanning is essential to knowing what services are running on a target system. It's part of information gathering and helps establish the attack surface.

Challenges

Scanning takes time, and some ports may be shielded behind a firewall that prevents detection.

Lesson learned. Ports act as doors, and anyone can attempt to access them. It's good practice for any organisation to lock down port-level security proactively.
