Maximum Devices Connected to a WiFi Router

When I joined campus, I was placed in a hostel with free WiFi. The connection was often slow, and it bothered me enough to investigate — especially since the connection was sometimes excellent.

I teamed up with hostelmates to figure out the maximum number of devices a LAN WiFi setup could realistically support. We connected devices one at a time, watching the connection as each new device joined.

As more devices came online, the connection lagged noticeably — until we hit roughly 10 connected devices, at which point performance dropped off sharply.

Lesson learned. For that specific LAN setup, around 10 devices appeared to be the practical ceiling before shared bandwidth became the bottleneck.
